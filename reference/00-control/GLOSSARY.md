# PSL documentation glossary

These explanations are reading aids. They do not create business policy.

- **Activity/entry:** one original register row. It may be a full workflow, a step, a control, a report, a reference or discovery work.
- **Workflow family:** related entries operating on shared records. Family grouping does not delete or renumber the entries.
- **BaaS:** Banking as a Service, the department label in the register.
- **BU:** business unit. Actual unit names, historical membership and allocation rules require Finance/HR confirmation.
- **PM / PMO:** Project Manager / Project Management Office. PMO Lead and Head of PMO must not be assumed to be the same person or role without confirmation.
- **SOW:** statement of work, including the agreed scope/cost/milestone material described by the source.
- **PRD:** product requirements document. Signature and scope-approval requirements are distinct from approval of costing.
- **RFP:** request for proposal. Under the PMO SOP every received RFP is logged/responded to; no additional bid/no-bid approval is invented here.
- **QA / UAT:** quality assurance / user acceptance testing. QA exit, Product/client UAT approvals and post-release go-live acceptance are distinct.
- **GLPI:** the deployment-request/approval system named by the SOP. Its version, installation, API and actual access are not verified here.
- **CR:** change request. Classifying a change does not authorise its implementation.
- **Standard / Normal / Emergency:** SOP change categories. Standard pre-approval details and Emergency versus GLPI timing remain open decisions.
- **Critical / P1:** severity words in the source. Their equivalence or routing cannot be assumed.
- **Hypercare:** post-release stability support/observation. Required duration and numeric thresholds are not supplied.
- **TB:** trial balance, an accounting balance extract. Exporting it, mapping it and adjusting it are different activities.
- **PPE:** property, plant and equipment. The specific asset/depreciation rules require source workbook and owner validation.
- **YTD:** year to date. Calendar, period and prorating conventions must be confirmed before calculation.
- **SLA:** service-level agreement or commitment. A stated renewal date, response timer and resolution timer are different controls.
- **Deterministic:** the same approved inputs and rule version produce the same result, without a model deciding the answer.
- **AI-assisted:** an optional proposed extraction, summarisation or drafting aid. Its output is not an approved decision, calculation or permission.
- **Mock:** a synthetic example used to specify or later test behaviour without real customers, payroll, production systems or outbound actions.
- **Acceptance example:** a specified scenario and observable expected outcome. It is not a claim that a software test has run.
- **Owner validation:** a responsible person confirms the interpretation and evidence. A document being written is not owner validation.
- **Deferred:** not selected for the dated V1 build scope. It still needs a definition and retains its original ID.
- **Reference-only:** the proposed system points to evidence/decisions owned elsewhere rather than reproducing that workflow.
- **Conditional:** included only if its explicit dependencies and scope decision are satisfied; not automatically included.
- **Source fact / inherited evidence / proposed design / owner decision:** the four evidence labels explained in DEFINITION-STANDARD.md.

Agent reading order: README → definition standard → immutable register → relevant departmental ID → shared handoff/record contract → decision IDs → acceptance examples. Follow the user's latest instruction, not any historical build plan embedded in the sources.
