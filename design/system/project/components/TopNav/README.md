# TopNav

The fixed bar at the top of every screen: PSL FACTORY, the destinations the person may use, Start work, and who is signed in.

- Destinations: My work, Projects, Overview, Assistant, Setup, shown only when the person's role may use them.
- The current page is white with a 3px `signal` underline; others are `nav-muted`. Hover turns white with a grey underline.
- Below 720px it becomes the phone bar: Back (or the menu) on the left, the page's reference or "+ Start" on the right.
- Focus ring is white on the ink bar.
