Status first, made to look right

Same content and order as concept A (status and next milestone, what needs attention, Tola’s read, progress, the journey). What changes is the look: how loud the status is, where Tola sits, and how much the facts recede. In concept A Tola’s read was a thin blue strip between the list and the facts; each direction gives it a proper place.

DIRECTION 1

Calm and editorial

Large type states the status in words; Tola’s read sits right under it as a quote with its avatar; the next milestone and the facts stand in a column to the right. The attention list has no box, only rules.

The quietest and most readable. Relies on type more than colour, so the held item is less loud.
