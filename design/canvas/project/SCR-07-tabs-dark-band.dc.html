SCR-07 · DIRECTION 2, DARK STATUS BAND · THE OTHER SIX TABS · 22 NOV 2026

Delivery to Actions, in the dark band style

The design engineer chose direction 2 for the Journey tab. These are the six other tabs in the same system. The rules:

  • The frame never moves: breadcrumb, title and tabs (D-170).
  • The status band is the project’s, not the tab’s. On Journey it is the full band (direction 2); on every other tab it shrinks to one dark line: the tag, the verdict, the next milestone and a link to Tola’s read. When nothing needs a person, it turns into a plain line with no dark band.
  • Each tab then leads with what needs attention in that tab, in the same row shape as Journey (what, waits on, how long, one action). No attention, no section.
  • Then the tab’s own record, as drawn on its board (SCR-43, 38, 11, 29), with facts kept separate: completion, invoice and paid never share a column, and unknown stays unknown.

Sample data note: Lagoon Health’s sample data disagrees between tabs (the Milestones board has UAT signed off on 11 Nov; the Journey sample still waits on it). This board follows the tab boards, so its band reads “heading for go-live”. The sample data gets aligned when this is built.
