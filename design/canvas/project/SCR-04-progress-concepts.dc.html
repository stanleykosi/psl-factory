How to show a line's stations

Same data in all three: the invoice line's 7 stations and who does each. Pick one and it replaces the current row of boxes on Start work, and is reused wherever a line's stations are previewed.

A

Track

The line as a rail of stations, like the factory floor. Strongest link to the factory idea; reads at a glance. Weaker at saying who acts, so it leans on colour and a short legend.

B

Lanes

Three lanes (people, agent, automatic) with each station in the lane of whoever does it. Best at showing handoffs between agents and people, which is this product's core rule. Busiest of the three; on a phone it becomes a small grid.

C

Steps with handoffs

A numbered list with a summary on top, and the moment work waits for a person marked as a handoff. Calmest and easiest to scan; identical on desktop and phone; works for long lines (some have 9+ stations).

Recommendation: C. The job of this panel is to answer "what happens after I press Start, and when will I be needed?" C answers that in one line of summary and makes the single person decision impossible to miss, and it works the same on a phone. A is the better fit for the factory floor overview, where it already lives.
