How Start work opens

Same case in all four: a PMO starts an invoice for a completed milestone. Line names are the backend's real ones; only lines the person can start are offered.

The trigger: a split button on My work

The main part starts your most-used line; the arrow opens the menu: lines you can start (only those your role allows), all lines, and "Describe it to Tola". Every concept below opens from here.

Drawer from the split button

The split button's menu lists the lines you use most; picking one slides a drawer in from the right with that line's short intake. My work stays visible behind it, and you never leave the page. On a phone it's a bottom sheet.

Describe it (agentic)

From the split button menu, or by just typing: you describe the work in plain words. Tola picks the line among those you can start, fills the intake from records it can read, and shows what happens next. You press Start; it never starts work for you.

From: Our Assistant (SCR-05) · Strawberry's companion (REF-17) · Ramp's visible agent fill-in (REF-09)
