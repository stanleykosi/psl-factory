What you see after approving

The built page scatters the result: a wide empty band, the next step squeezed into a narrow column, the button floating on its own, and the paid note, back link and decider stacked as loose lines. Each concept below keeps the same facts (what you decided, what was earned, what happens next, where to go) and orders them differently. Same example: the CEO approves Costing v1 for Ojo Foods.

CONCEPT A

Receipt

One centred column, read top to bottom like a receipt: the result, what you decided, the facts, what happens next, then the two ways on.

Calm and complete. The width is fixed, so wide screens keep empty sides.
