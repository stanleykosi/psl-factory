Replacing the floor strip on the queue

Floor briefing (agentic)

Tola and Chidi each brief you on their lines in plain words, citing the records, and offer buttons that only open the item. They never act for you. It turns the floor into "what changed and what needs me", which is what agentic AI is good at.
