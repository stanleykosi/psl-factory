# Screen inventory

Every screen the approved journeys need, with its states and its artboards on the
canvas. One line per screen; IDs stay stable for the life of the project, and
delivery tasks reference them.

**Early start, recorded 12 Nov 2026.** These screens are being drawn before the
architecture stage, on realistic sample data, on the explicit instruction of the
design engineer (hunchoweb). The architecture package (the data API agreed with the
backend developer, assumption A-01) has not been produced yet, so each screen's
data, permissions and failure states are taken from the current backend's records
and rules and must be checked and revised once architecture is approved. This is
not a client approval and does not replace the architecture gate.

Concept: CON-C Assembly line, design system "PSL Factory". Agents: Tola (PMO),
Chidi (Finance), Zainab (HR).

**Every screen and every state has both a desktop and a phone version** (design engineer, 12 Nov 2026; serves C-01). The alert's desktop counterpart is the email alert.

Format: `- SCR-n | J-n[, J-n] | Screen title | states: … | components: CMP-n, … | artboards: …`

## Data each screen shows

Sample data until the architecture package exists. Sources are the current backend's records, named in plain terms; the API endpoints come from architecture.

- SCR-01 — work items the person may see, with line, current station, state, who it waits for, age; queue counts; floor counts per line.
- SCR-02 — one work item: stations from its line definition, the six facts, agent steps and the records they read, the current human task and decision, send jobs and receipts.
- SCR-03 — one human task: the exact version, amount and basis from contract terms, recipient, what changed, who may decide.
- SCR-04 — the lines this person may start and each line's intake fields.
- SCR-05 — conversations, the records an answer cites, drafts and the work items they start; the person's access per project.
- SCR-06 — every line, its stations and the count of items at each.
- SCR-07 — one project's work items across all lines, in order.
- SCR-08 — a document version, its template (test template for now), review state and audience.
- SCR-09 — an imported file, its rows, mapping and the rows that failed checks.
- SCR-10 — items with an uncertain send or a failed check, and what is being done about each.
- SCR-11 — issues, change requests, emergencies and RFPs with owners and states.
- SCR-12 — a report's figures and, for each, the records it came from.
- SCR-13 — roles and who holds them, rules with versions, delegations; only for roles allowed to change them.
- SCR-14 — verified outputs with their facts and receipts.
- SCR-15 — alerts sent to the person, their channel choices and quiet hours.
- SCR-16 — the step waiting for an outside response, the response, the uploaded proof and who recorded it.
- SCR-17 — sign-in only.
- SCR-18 — payroll inputs, staff-matching results and what this role may see.
- SCR-19 — the milestone from the signed SOW, completion date, the evidence record and proof, who confirmed it.
- SCR-20 — issued invoices with amounts invoiced, received and still owed (Unknown where bank data is missing), overdue age, receipts with bank reference and proof, and allocations kept separate from receipts.
- SCR-21 — staff accounts with roles and scopes, account state (invited, active, deactivated), last sign-in; on deactivation, the person's open items and who may take each over.
- SCR-22 — projects the person's roles cover, with PM, where each stands, next milestone, and counts waiting on a person or stopped.
- SCR-23 — RFP reference, client, RFP document, actual received time, deadline (unknown / stated with proof / not stated), note; creates a pre-engagement workspace (backend: rfp.intake).
- SCR-24 — the unassigned RFP, PMs allowed to prepare RFPs on it, the role the assigner acts under (PSL-configured), proof, note (backend: rfp.assign).
- SCR-25 — the week's meetings, actions, completed milestones, open issues and changes; the PM's four sections, each citing at least one record (backend: client content for pmo-weekly-report).
- SCR-26 — RFP requirements matrix (up to 100 rows: requirement, RFP page, our response, addressed / needs clarification / out of scope), team input status, reviewers (backend: rfp.prepare, rfp.review).
- SCR-27 — costing inputs (requirements, plan, engineering and security estimates, PSL costing rule), calculation from the rule, total Unknown while any estimate is missing, approval order PM → Head of PMO → CEO → PMO Lead (backend: commercial line).
- SCR-28 — meeting, notes evidence, agent-suggested decisions and actions (owner, due date), audience; nothing becomes a decision or action until confirmed (backend: pmo-minutes confirm, meeting.record).
- SCR-29 — the project's action items with owner (or none), due date (or Unknown), state and the meeting they came from.
- SCR-30 — issue facts, history, owner follow-up (acknowledge / started / progress only; backend: issue.work).
- SCR-31 — change stages, impact assessments by team (recorded by PM), decision roles, agent-drafted notice (backend: pmo-change).
- SCR-33 — retrospective sections citing project records, participants, closure checklist (closure prerequisite).
- SCR-38 — milestones from the signed SOW: contract amount (or Unknown), completion and proof, invoice state, paid state — each a separate fact.
- SCR-39 — the studio-wide costing rule: inputs, priced parameters each with its source, calculation steps, worked examples with match results, rule and examples owners (different people), versions.
- SCR-40 — submission pack drafted by Tola from the approved response and costing (cover letter, forms), checks, document reviewer decision, submit by the configured publisher and receipt check; no receipt → Effect uncertain (backend: rfp line steps 5–7).
- SCR-41 — engagement from a won RFP (route A): Tola's planning packet with Unknowns kept, PM confirmed (pre-filled from the RFP), registration with plan evidence that creates the project and starts planning (backend: engagement line).
- SCR-32 — overdue invoice facts and an agent-drafted reminder that a Finance person edits and sends; delivery checked after sending.
- SCR-34 — document templates per line and whether each is a test template or PSL's own; versioned uploads.
- SCR-35 — client register (projects, billing contact, readiness) and client details (billing contact needed before invoices start).
- SCR-36 — month-end checklist from the finance lines' current records, with Unknown where data is missing.
- SCR-37 — HR correction requests (restricted; identities masked) and the HR Lead decision (backend: payroll.hr_correction).
