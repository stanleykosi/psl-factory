Engineering contract implemented for the declared registry:

1. Build a closed adapter registry of actual domain records that pin a local Configuration directly or in typed policy-pin payloads. Define current/pending/historical meaning per model and module, with exact domain read actions. No generic unrestricted ORM traversal, payload export, hidden totals or guessed workflow status.
2. Resolve source → explicitly applied configurations → authorized affected records. Preserve unknown coverage rather than claiming every JSON or indirect dependency has been discovered. Paginate and bound records without silently calling a partial sample complete. Include immutable policy/source version IDs and current origin status.
3. Add an append-only engineering reassessment record linking an exact old local configuration, explicitly selected candidate successor, actual observed affected-record heads and human disposition/evidence. Recording this assessment is not a business approval, actor appointment, financial restatement or effect retry.
4. Recheck source/target authority, exact policy and record heads under existing service locks. Stale assessments hold only dependent review completion. New domain decisions/snapshots must use the relevant domain successor services, not an update to old records or an automatic copy of an old approval.
5. Keep effect recovery receipt-first. Distinguish pending work that may be reassessed, uncertain effects requiring reconciliation and confirmed effects whose evidence remains authoritative. No scope lifecycle change may schedule outbound effects or replay archived jobs.
6. Add a bounded A04 investigation over that authorized projection and an ordinary human interface with source links, explicit unknowns and observed-head conflicts. Test actual PostgreSQL PMO/Finance/BaaS examples, cross-case privacy, stale and revoked authority, concurrent assessment, no effect replay, and real receipt reconciliation. Preserve all122 original IDs and their separate acceptance statuses.

These choices are engineering design implementing guide10, not owner policy. Coverage outside the declared registry remains unknown: other policy consumers and indirect dependencies, controlled-document audiences, private Finance/HR/payroll/BU domains and invoice-correction receipt variants.
