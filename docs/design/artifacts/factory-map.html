The model

One work item, three kinds of actor

Every piece of work follows the same pattern. Agents carry it forward on their own and stop only for what the rules reserve to a person. Services do the arithmetic and the sending, and only the verification service can call the work done.

The rule behind every screen: each of these is a separate fact

Drafted→ Reviewed→ Approved→ Sent→ Receipt recorded→ Verified Paid

The UI can never show one of these as another. A successful agent run is not an approval, and an API acknowledgement is not a customer receipt. Payment is its own Finance fact: a verified invoice says nothing about whether it was paid.

Scope

122 processes in 13 families, 108 in this release

Every original business process keeps its ID (P for PMO, F for Finance, B for BaaS). This release covers PMO and Finance. The 14 BaaS (Banking-as-a-Service) processes already have code, which is kept, but they're deferred. The bar is drawn to scale, one unit per process.

The main line

One client project, from opportunity to closure

The PMO line runs across the top. Finance runs underneath and receives handoffs at fixed points: PMO completes milestones, Finance invoices. Costing and detailed planning run in parallel, and both finish before the client signs. Select a station to jump to its details.

01 · Route A only

RFP opportunity

Built line · Prepare an RFP response
  1. P45Log every RFP and assign a PM.
  2. P46Analyse requirements, shape the solution, estimate effort and cost.
  3. P47Coordinate the technical and commercial response.
  4. P48Get costing sign-off, then submit the response.
  5. P49Won: becomes a Route A project. Lost: archived with lessons.

People decide

  • PMO Lead and Engineering Lead review the response, in either order.
  • PM → Head of PMO → CEO sign off the costing, in that order.
  • A person records the real customer outcome.

Agents triage the RFP, investigate requirements, draft the response and line up reviews. They can't infer a win or a loss.

02

Engagement & requirements

No dedicated line yet
  1. P16Log the engagement with its route: A (won RFP), B (direct client) or C (product paper). Assign the PM.
  2. P03Register the project and link its identifiers.
  3. P17Define requirements and map which teams must take part.

No approval here

  • Phase gates and client signatures come later.

Agents spot duplicate engagements and identity conflicts, extract requirements and list the gaps.

03 · parallel with 04

Costing & SOW

Built line · Prepare a commercial submission
  1. P18Collect Engineering and Security estimates.
  2. P06Build resource-driven costing.
  3. P07Prepare SOW costing and milestones.
  4. P09Route the costing for approval.
  5. P19Submit to the client; handle feedback and revisions.
  6. P08A cost-changing revision runs the approval chain again.

People decide

  • PM → Head of PMO → CEO, strictly in that order.
  • PMO Lead quality-reviews the document before it leaves (P53).

Agents interpret estimates, draft the costing and SOW and chase missing estimates. Amounts come from configured rules, never from agent arithmetic.

04 · parallel with 03

Detailed planning

Built line · Plan a project
  1. P21Two PRD alignment sessions to close scope and dependency gaps.
  2. P22–24Sign-offs: UI design, technical design, test strategy and UAT approach.
  3. P25Scope and estimate baselines.
  4. P26Detailed plan and Planning exit; milestones go to Finance.

People decide

  • Design Lead (and Client where applicable), Engineering Lead, QA Lead, Product Owner.
  • PMO Lead approves the plan.

Planning happens before the client signs. A signed SOW is not a prerequisite.

Agents collect each sign-off reference, track what's missing and prepare the handoff.

05

Signed baseline

No dedicated line yet
  1. P20The client signs both the SOW and the PRD.
  2. P12The PM shares the signed costs and milestones with Finance.

People decide

  • The client signs both documents.

Finance's opening billing handoff needs this signature. No deposit or payment gate is implied.

Agents extract the signed references and flag missing or conflicting milestones.

06

Kick-off & delivery

No dedicated line yet
  1. P27Kick-off with Product, Engineering, QA, Infra and Security; minutes and actions.
  2. P28Track delivery, dependencies, blockers and scope changes.
  3. P02Route work and show other teams' approvals without taking them over.
  4. P05A permissioned shared project view for PMO, management and Engineering.

No new approval

  • Engineering and QA work under their own processes.

Agents track blockers, chase overdue inputs and open issues or change requests in their own flows.

07

Release gates

Built line · Prepare for release
  1. P29QA exit with no critical defects.
  2. P30UAT validated and approvals collected.
  3. P31Engineering pre-production Go / No-Go.
  4. P32GLPI deployment request approved; Infra/DevOps deploys.

People decide

  • QA Lead signs QA exit.
  • Product Manager, Product Owner and client contact sign UAT, in any order.
  • Security, CTO and CEO approve GLPI, in any order.

Agents assemble QA, UAT, Engineering and GLPI evidence for the exact build and flag version mismatches.

08

Go-live

Built line · Obtain go-live acceptance
  1. P33Post-release go-live and business-acceptance sign-off.

People decide

  • The PM and the Client both sign.
  • A PM attests that the signatures are real.

Agents investigate the go-live inputs, prepare the client report, request review and deliver it. After the PM's attestation, the agent verifies the acceptance.

09

Hypercare

Built line · Follow up a release
  1. P35Confirm stability after release, before closure.

People decide

  • Engineering or Infrastructure confirms stability.

No numeric threshold is prescribed. The criteria have to be configured.

Agents check current observations against the reviewed period and metrics. Unknown or negative results stay held.

10

Archive & handover

Built lines · Archive records · Hand over to Support
  1. P36Engineering finalizes the technical docs; PMO archives the project artifacts.
  2. P37Hand over to Support and Operations with escalation documentation.

No approval

  • The SOP explicitly says handover needs no approval.

Agents check the declared inventory, prepare the operating instructions and track the recipient's receipt or the gaps.

11

Closure

Built line · Close a project
  1. P38Retrospective and improvement actions.
  2. P39The PM prepares the closure report, Finance confirms the milestone notifications, then closure is approved and archived.

People decide

  • The Head of PMO approves closure.
  • Finance confirms notification coverage. This is not a financial approval.

Closure has no "invoice paid" gate.

Agents prepare the PM report, wait for the reviews, then verify the recorded closure.

Recurring · Finance

Milestones & invoicing

Built line · Prepare an invoice
  1. P34The PM records a milestone as complete and notifies Finance.
  2. F29The Finance service calculates the amount from the contract stage.
  3. F30The invoice is generated, reviewed, issued and delivered (mock today).
  4. F32Paid and unpaid tracking, plus collection follow-up.

People decide

  • A Finance reviewer approves the exact amount, version and recipient.

Stages, invoices, receipts and allocations stay separate records. Verified delivery records no payment.

Agents gather evidence, investigate holds, draft the invoice and reconcile uncertain sends.

Running alongside every project

Changes, reporting and document control

These run in parallel with the main line rather than as stations on it. Change requests are the most important for design, because emergencies flip the usual order.

Issues, risks and escalation

P50 issue lifecycle·P51 Security risk register·P54–55 production incidents·P44 client escalation

Issues have owners, next actions and timestamps. Risks and incidents are referenced from Security and Engineering, never copied into a PMO master. PMO Lead or Head of PMO approves escalating to a client, and approves de-escalating too.

Reporting rhythm

P40 Tuesday PMO meetingP41 weekly client reportP42 monthly exec reportP43 formal minutes

Agents draft the summaries and reports. Anything going to a client passes PMO Lead review first. Minutes record decisions, actions, owners and deadlines.

Document control

P10 template→P52 versioned draft→P53 quality review→audience version→publication receipt

Every external document passes P53. The approval is tied to the exact reviewed version and its audience. A recurring report never gets automatic permission to go to a new audience.

Evidence and validation

P13 PMO SOPP14 pricing calculatorP15 validate with process users

This family collects missing SOPs and calculator examples and gets rule owners to validate them. Discovery is real workflow here, and nothing gets automated away.

Finance's own cycle

Month-end: trial balance to cash forecast

This line isn't driven by a project. It starts from QuickBooks every month. Services do every calculation from owner-approved rules; agents investigate the differences and explain the results.

Management accounts

  4. F07Assets and depreciation

The reference line

The invoice line, step by step

This is the line the backend uses to prove the whole architecture, and it's the best one to design first. The happy path runs left to right. Every loop above and below it is a state the console has to name and show.

Work states

What the console says about a work item today

These are the state labels the queue and work item page currently render, grouped by who has to act next. "Running" is deliberately absent: an item waiting on a person must say so.

Moving

Ready to continue

Eligible work can run now.

Processing

A worker holds this item right now.

Waiting on a person

Waiting for information

A task asks someone for one specific missing fact.

Awaiting Finance decision

A reviewer has an exact packet to approve, return or reject.

Held

Action needed

A required source, rule or permission needs review.

Owner needed

No eligible owner is configured. Only the dependent action waits.

Setup needed

A valid execution profile is missing, so agent work can't start.

Uncertain

Effect uncertain

A send may have happened. Reconcile before any resend.

Awaiting reconciliation

The service is checking for the original by the same key.

Checking

Awaiting verification

Effects are recorded; receipts are being checked against the approved version.

Ended

Verified

Every required receipt matched.

Cancellation requested · Cancelled

New work stops. A sent invoice can't be undone.

Stopped; invoice follow-up needed

Work stopped after an invoice went out; Finance owns the follow-up.

Interaction states the spec also requires

LoadingKeeps useful context on screen while it loads.
EmptySays whether there's no work or the filters hide it.
Source unavailableNever shown as zero work.
StaleThe record changed while you reviewed it. Shows what changed.
DeniedExplains the permitted next step without leaking restricted detail.
ConflictSomeone else acted first. Unsent notes are kept.
Retry requestedA request, not proof of a fix.

Agent crew

One coordinator, eight specialists

Roles are reused across all 108 processes, so there isn't one agent per process. Each role works through a narrow set of typed tools. The console shows what an agent read, did and produced, never its private reasoning.

O01

Coordinator

Picks the next allowed task, delegates to specialists, waits for people and replans when the evidence changes.

Resumes the invoice line after PMO supplies a missing reference.

A01

Intake & triage

Turns messy intake into a case and flags duplicates and unclear matches.

Logs a new RFP; spots a duplicate engagement.

A02

Evidence & documents

Extracts facts with citations, compares versions and finds contradictions.

Pulls the signed milestone references out of the SOW.

A03

Exception investigation

Traces a discrepancy to missing data, stale state, a conflict or an unknown effect.

Works out why a receivable doesn't match.

A04

Planning & coordination

Tracks dependencies and opens the right internal tasks.

Finds the next reviewer in the PM → Head of PMO → CEO chain.

A05

Finance analysis

Explains the service's calculations and investigates mapping, sign, unit and allocation problems.

Explains a company-to-BU difference.

A06

Insight & drafting

Writes grounded summaries, reports and decision briefs, with uncertainty stated.

Drafts the weekly client status report.

A07

Control evidence

Checks that the required sign-off evidence is complete and matches the exact version.

Assembles QA, UAT and GLPI proof for one build.

A08

Quality review

Critiques outputs against independent test cases and the source evidence.

Reviews a draft before a person sees it.

None of them can approve, set a price or rule, decide a win or loss, send anything outside the company without authority, or mark work verified. Text inside a document can't grant them authority either.

Open questions

Decisions to settle before designing

The spec leaves these open, and each one changes the layout or navigation.

  • Workspaces or work items?A project (workspace) spans many work items across several lines. The nav carries both today. Which one is the main object people navigate by?
  • Who sees shared queues?What can a supervisor see across teams without widening their access to client evidence?
  • Where do deadlines come from?None are configured, and the spec forbids inventing them. Which clocks pause while work waits on an outside party?
  • The stage trailHow do we show retries, parallel branches, rework and failures as one readable history, without erasing any attempt?
  • The 35 department toolsWhich fold into the work item view, and which stay as reference pages?
  • Confirmation weightWhich actions need consequence wording before you confirm, and which can be a single click?
