Operational identity/provisioning/outbound activation remains separate engineering. Isolated application recovery and independently authorised cold recovery do not confer operational activation. Existing unconditional startup quarantine has no bypass. Do not change it to a configuration checkbox or treat a restored approval as live authority.

Build a separate fresh-authority reconstitution workflow that captures the exact physically verified recovery, compatible reviewed application/migration inventory, intended operational identity/store/database references and unresolved effects. Preserve current source ownership and PMO/Finance/BaaS responsibilities. Archived users, grants, sessions, pending jobs and human decisions are historical evidence. An operator must not reactivate archived credentials or drain restored queues.

Implement typed versioned criteria, validated explicit purpose/role assignments, independent human review and visible holds for dependent actions. Unknown policy values remain empty. Build a bounded agent that investigates restored pending/unknown effects and drafts a reconciliation/activation plan without dispatching business work. Expose source-specific effect inventories through typed domain adapters, with record/action privacy and no archive bodies in shared diagnostics. Require independently verified external receipts or human dispositions before enabling each affected adapter; unknown outcomes must remain unknown rather than be retried blindly.

Operational identity/session/credential creation, outbound enablement and target provisioning require concrete independently reviewed deployment contracts and actual access. Design any activation adapter around those reviewed contracts; do not guess production topology, rename/drop existing databases, change real grants, invent an approver or add a recovered-startup bypass while requirements are undecided. Keep bounded inventory/planning/review behavior available before those deployment actions can run.
