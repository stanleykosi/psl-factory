# Railway trial decisions for workflow owners

These decisions add detail to the department-head questions. The trial uses a synthetic case.

- **Project registration.** Who assigns the project manager and issues the official project number? Which register owns that number and how should duplicates be resolved?
- **Evidence and quality.** Who confirms what a source means, who signs off the expected result, and who independently accepts the workflow? Provide one normal and one exception example with expected results.
- **Issue closure.** Who records the fix and who supplies the quality and project-management closure entries? Which source proves the fix was checked?
- **Planning.** Who owns the scope and estimate decisions in each department? Which records prove that the two alignment meetings and the required approvals took place?
- **Finance recognition.** Who attests the project evidence, who receives it, and who can independently record the Finance decision? Which signed baseline and price evidence must accompany the progress record?
- **Reports.** Who may receive each report, who reviews it before it leaves the team, and which template and delivery channel should be used?
- **Release evidence.** Which system is the source for each release check, and who verifies the exact build and release? Which receipt confirms that the release happened?
- **Agent data.** Which document types may be sent to the model provider, who approves access and retention, and who owns the budget and reviews held jobs?
