# Supervised policy-impact pilot — proposed operating sequence

This is a pilot runbook, not evidence of a completed live run. Owner policy values remain deferred. No operational users, grants, configurations, credentials or effects are installed by the build.

1. Use a separate controlled pilot deployment, retained PostgreSQL/storage, worker visibility and verified backup/recovery. Authenticate actual participants and explicitly grant only the chosen case/actions. Keep unverified outbound integrations unavailable.
2. Choose one planning/review project whose required policy and source evidence are available. Record unknowns explicitly; unrelated modules remain usable when another rule is missing. Avoid treating synthetic role or financial examples as operational values.
3. Publish/apply a deliberately selected source policy if reuse is intended. Validate and activate the exact target-local policy through the existing human configuration interface. Preserve the prior version and actual decisions.
4. Inspect selected PMO impact adapters, capture an observation and have a second authorized person review it. Demonstrate rejection of a stale head, missing source/domain access and self-review. Confirm no business decision or job is created by the review.
5. Reassess actual affected work using its domain services. Verify old approvals do not carry forward. Add Finance/BaaS adapters only when their actual contracts and required policies have been verified for the pilot.
6. For an uncertain effect, use the existing operator recovery interface to read its receipt before any retry. Inspect its impact record before/after reconciliation and confirm the original assessment becomes stale without receipt loss or duplicate effect.
7. Record staff acceptance, policy versions, exact source contracts, held operations, actual receipt meanings and recovery/security observations. Keep uncovered adapters and untested variants visible. A successful selected pilot is not acceptance of all122 processes or full production readiness.
