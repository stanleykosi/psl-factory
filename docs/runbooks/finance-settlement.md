## Cash

Capture cash_report evidence as exact JSON source,transaction_ref,customer,amount,currency,amount_basis,received_at. Use null for unknown amount; otherwise positive finite exact decimal, max8 fractional places. Actual timestamp must be aware and not future. Bank/source transaction namespace is tenant/source/transaction, SQL-unique across cases. Same-case identical facts return the original; changed facts or another case require authorised reconciliation, never another cash lot. No foreign record amount/ID is disclosed. Supplied source locators are assertions, not source-system authentication.

D14.receipt_verification_source_ref cash-source/v1 chooses exact source/currency/gross basis/precision and independent declared locator requirement. D14.recorder_authority_ref cash-recorder/v1 chooses allowed human roles and explicit reporter-verifier separation. Verification must match immutable reported known facts with cash_verification evidence; current actor grants and role assignment are locked/rechecked. A separate locator is required only if configured; even this is not bank authenticity. Unknown/unverified reports never increase cash or settle debt. Existing verified cash is historical evidence across ordinary later policy changes until an explicit full reversal.

D14.allocation_rule_ref cash-matching/v1 selects explicit_human_v1, roles, verifier-allocator separation, reassignment, precision and due_date_before_as_of ageing. Assign a receipt to one explicit same-customer contract, or leave unmatched. Allocation requires verified unreversed cash, proven invoice issuance, matching customer/contract/currency/basis, a semantic operation key and both current capacities in one transaction. Multiple receipts/invoices are allowed within the same contract; no FIFO default or automatic agent matching. Fractional precision is validated, never rounded silently. Excess cash stays unallocated; unassigned funds cannot count against a contract.

D14.adjustment_correction_rule_refs cash-reversal/v1 and D14.legal_document_requirements cash-legal-evidence/v1 select exact lot/allocation targets, human roles/separation, full_once_v1, required cash_correction evidence and written reason. Full allocation reversal reopens debt without removing cash. Full lot reversal atomically reverses all live allocations and removes the lot once, preserving every original record. Reassignment reverses existing live allocations under current correction authority before new contract ownership commits. Any missing policy/authority rolls back the whole action. Partial refunds/chargebacks, credits/write-offs and corrected transaction successor lots need distinct future implementations; none is silently represented as a full reversal.

## Receivables and bounded investigation

Build current-day ReceivableSnapshot over every captured case record; caller subsets and unsupported historical as-of dates cannot certify balance. Manifest includes issue/receipt/lot/assignment/allocation/reversal IDs, exact invoice hashes, job status/fence and age-rule version. Issued open debt, known overdue terms, unbilled drafts, unknown outcomes, unmatched verified cash and reported-unverified queues remain distinct by currency/basis. Negative capacity becomes a conflict, never clamped. No contract cash target, payment percentage, deployment authorization or whole-company forecast is inferred. UI marks old/fact-changed snapshots historical; agents also check actual date/freshness.

A03/A04/A05/A07 deterministic investigation cites source/usage/pricing oracle/review/cash/correction evidence hashes and current dimensions. Matching alternatives are individually bounded by available cash/open debt, explicitly alternatives rather than a batch, requiring a configured human to execute. No posting/approval/allocation/waiver/dispatch tools. No real-provider evaluation or live bank result is claimed.
