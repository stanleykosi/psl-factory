# Synthetic development fixtures

The initial planning-to-Finance fixture uses fictional people/customer/artifacts. Unknown commercial amounts, stage terms and payment basis deliberately remain null. This tests missing-policy behavior, not a restriction on implementing complete Finance functionality.

Add each family's normal, error, concurrency and recovery examples and each policy slot's absent/invalid/synthetic-active/stale/revoked cases. Use separately identified synthetic configurations to prove configured paths, including human exception infrastructure, without pretending they establish real organizational authority or accounting policy. Keep fixture scope explicit; operational policy lookup never falls back to synthetic records.

The known 80% expectation can be tested with independently labelled synthetic denominator/qualification rules; actual assessment still requires its own approved basis. Preserve exact decimal/currency/units and independent expected outputs. Technical test clocks and execution budgets are reliability knobs, not business SLA or production policy.
