## Payroll visibility: what depends on D-PAYROLL-VISIBILITY (open)

D-PAYROLL-VISIBILITY is open (owner: business owner). The SCR-13 "fields" board shows Finance seeing Staff ID and Business unit as "Visible" and Gross pay, Deductions, Bank account, Home address, Date of birth as "Hidden", with the HR Lead able to change this per field. `E-PAYROLL-MATCH` is today flat "HR roles only" and INV-RESTRICTED says only roles with payroll private read get rows. Until PSL decides, treat every field below as HR-only and return nothing about it to anyone else (not even a count, a label or a disabled option):

- SCR-18: staff name, staff ID (`P-0388`), payroll amount, HR record text ("active", "Left 30 Sep"), business unit per person, per-row result.
- SCR-09: file names with uploader, row counts per file (142, 139), problem rows (row number, staff ID text such as "P-0412 appears twice"), the "140 rows" figure.
- SCR-37: correction text, masked staff reference (`S-●●●41`; the masking rule is itself a visibility decision), proof (transfer letter), requester, state.
- SCR-59: every input's row count and checks, gross pay total of the combined set (₦99.2M + Unknown), match counts (136 / 2 / 1 / 2), BU staff counts. Totals by business unit are the board's own "only these aggregates leave HR" set; whether the staff count per BU is also an aggregate Finance may see is a D-PAYROLL-VISIBILITY question.
- SCR-04 payroll and BU drawers: option labels such as "Accepted · 142 rows", "Oct snapshot · sent to you", "PAY-0010 · October".
- SCR-18 Finance view: "Delivery 48 / ₦31,200,000" shows staff count and cost per BU to Finance; "Staff ID" and "Business unit" toggles on SCR-13 make this configurable.
- Receipt state ("Finance Lead RECEIVED") and recipient names on SCR-59 are not payroll data, but recipient list membership is "Reporting rules / Who reviews payroll" (SCR-13 finance rules, all NOT SET).

Rule for the backend developer: field visibility is decided per record and action on the server (INV-ACCESS); a field that a viewer may not see is absent or `restricted: true`, never zero and never a placeholder that leaks existence. Setup values that are NOT SET (Who may read inputs, Merge rules, Match rules, Reporting rules, Who reviews payroll) hold only the dependent runtime step (`setup_needed`), they are not defaulted.
