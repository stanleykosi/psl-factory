# Board-vs-contract gaps, group C: client lines (SCR-25 returned and received, SCR-28, SCR-31, SCR-50, SCR-51, SCR-52)

Checked 15 Nov 2026 against v7 (`architecture/packages/v7`, openapi.json, endpoints.csv, model.json, delivery.md) and the built frame (`delivery/features/F-17/spec.md`, `frontend/src/features/workspace/`). Every board of these screens was read, desktop and mobile, all states. Read-only; nothing else was edited.

How to read this file. All six screens are line workspaces: they read `E-WORK-ITEM` `GET /api/v1/work/{work_id}` (the `workspace` block, D-LINE-WORKSPACE, still `proposed`) and write through `E-WORK-STEP` `POST /api/v1/work/{work_id}/steps`, `E-TASK-CLAIM` + `E-TASK-DECIDE` for a human decision (a step with `task_id`), `E-RESPONSE` for something recorded for a party who does not log in, and `E-WORK-ACTION` for reconcile. Starting each line is `E-LINES` / `E-WORK-START` (already in the Start work drawer: line keys `weekly_report`, `minutes`, `change_request`, `emergency_change`, `escalation`, `leadership_report`). "Covered as is" means an existing section kind, step field kind or operation carries it. The write-side guards on every step are the F-17 ones (CSRF, `expected_revision` = `workspace.revision`, one `idempotency_key` per attempt, no client retry, `effect_uncertain` reconciled through `E-WORK-ACTION`), so they are not repeated per step.

Proposals shared by several screens are written out once, at the first screen that needs them, and referenced by id afterwards:
- X-LINKS (G-SCR28-3): boards end in "Open actions", "Back to the issue", "Open the report", "Open milestones", "Raise a new emergency change" and the workspace has no way to say where a button goes.
- X-STEP-OPTIONS (G-SCR28-4): option lists for fields inside a step are keyed by line and field only, with no work item.
- X-RECORD-FOR (G-SCR31-1): recording a decision, assessment, authorisation or signature for someone who does not log in.
- X-VERDICTS (G-SCR51-2): the board's own button words on a decision ("Escalate to the client", "Don't escalate", "Confirm outcome").
- X-CLIENT-SEND (G-SCR31-5): boards where the agent "publishes" to a client with no human review step drawn.
