How to read this

Each card is one decision. It shows:

  • The decision: the question PSL must answer, in bold at the top.
  • What it means: why it matters and what changes once it is answered.
  • Where it's taken: the screen, Setup page or meeting; the PSL role that owns it; the process IDs it affects.
  • On hold until then: the exact operation that waits. Everything else keeps working.
  • Status: Open Partly answered Answered, awaiting PSL confirmation
  • Basis: From PSL's documents means the question comes from PSL's own SOPs, testimony or confirmed clarifications. Engineering proposal awaiting PSL means the build team raised it, so it is not a PSL fact.

Owner roles are roles, never named people. Where a card says “proposed owner”, PSL should confirm the role. Sample names, amounts and roles on design boards are not answers. The grey Reference line lists the internal IDs, so the build team can trace each answer.
