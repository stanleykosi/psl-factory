## Findings
- UX-1-1 | blocking | high | builder | Saved cites are lost when the form opens. On W-2201 the four text areas prefill with the saved words, but all four "Cites for ..." pickers open empty. Pressing "Save draft" or "Send to Tola to assemble" then sends no `*_cites` at all (I recorded the W-2202 request: `inputs` holds only status, risks, timeline, next_steps). A PM who saved cited parts would silently erase the cites, and Send would draw a "cite at least one record" 422 for parts that were already cited. Spec AC 7 says the form shows "the saved values" for the cites too, and the page's own "Your update" text shows them as cited. build.md names this as a contract limit (records `value` is a string), but the fixture/mock path can still carry the cites (or the form must not offer a Save that drops them). Fix it in the frame (prefill from the item's saved cites) or put a visible line in the form saying the cites are kept, and record the decision. Without that, the journey "write four cited parts, send" is not usable.
- UX-1-2 | non-blocking | medium | builder | Every field in the weekly form is labelled "(optional)" ("Status (optional)", "Cites for risks (optional)"), while the page note and the 422 say every part needs words and a cite. The two messages contradict each other. The spec set `required: false` on purpose so the server owns the rule, so this is the descriptor's label, not a bug. Suggest the fixture label drop "(optional)" for these eight fields, or the frame stop adding the word when the descriptor says nothing. Raise with the design engineer.
- UX-1-3 | non-blocking | medium | builder | Disabled primary buttons read as enabled and are unreadable. "Send for PMO Lead review" (W-2203) and "Approve and send" (W-2205, locked) show a solid dark green fill with near-invisible dark label text. `aria-disabled` and a reason sentence are present, so axe passes, but a sighted user sees a strong green button with no legible label. Disabled primary should look like the grey disabled Return button beside it (W-2205 shows both side by side).
- UX-1-4 | non-blocking | medium | builder | Phone, form open (390): the sticky bar ("Send to Tola to assemble" and "Save draft", with the consequence sentence) stays fixed over the form, covers the Risks field while scrolling, and the form's own Send button repeats the same action at the bottom. Two Send buttons for one action on a narrow screen. Suggest the bar hides while the form is open, or the bar is the form's submit.
- UX-1-5 | non-blocking | low | builder | W-2205 at 1440 (uncertain send): the reconcile panel sits in the right column with a large empty area to its left, then a full-width "See all exceptions" bar, then the report and the Decide panel below. The page works (all steps locked, the reason "Confirm what happened first." shown) but the order is awkward: the report the PMO Lead is deciding on is pushed below the fold. This comes from the shared F-09/F-17 reconcile layout, so it is a note for the frame owner, not for F-21.
- UX-1-6 | non-blocking | low | builder | Phone titles and back links differ from the boards ("Write this week's client update" vs "Client update", "Queue" vs "My work"); "Weekly client report · 5–11 Nov" wraps to two lines on the phone review page. All were approved exceptions (D-71 Conflict 5). Noted only.
