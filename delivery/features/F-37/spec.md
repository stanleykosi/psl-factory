## What is sent, and what stays held (read first)
- **On the contract mock, nothing is sent to anyone.** No answer reaches the client, no reviewer is asked, and nothing is submitted. Every "addressed", "accepted" and "suggested" word on these boards is fixture text, worded as a backend would return it. The PR and `build.md` must say so and must not claim a working RFP response.
- **What the page does write (to the mock only):** four E-WORK-STEP steps named by the backend: `accept` (one row), `accept_selected` (the ticked rows), `edit_accept` (one row, the PM's edited answer) and `write_own` (one row, the PM's own answer and an internal reason). Also `add_requirement` when the response returns it. Each write is one deliberate press, carries CSRF, `expected_revision` = `workspace.revision` and one `idempotency_key` per attempt, and is followed by a re-read. The page never says "accepted" or "saved" until the 200 and the re-read say so.
- **Every answer is recorded under the PM's own name**, one by one, even in a bulk accept. That is the backend's promise; the page shows the response's message and the re-read rows, and claims nothing more.
- **Held, because a product or backend decision is open:**
  1. **Send for review** (part 2; G-26-9 and D-RETURN). Drawn only as the response sends it: here disabled, with the backend's reason. Pressing it sends nothing.
  2. **Pricing** (FE-COSTING, gated by D-COSTING-RULE). The Pricing tab stays plain text. No amount, rate, costing version or approver appears anywhere on these pages.
  3. **Which tab is which page** (G-26-1). This spec decides it for the mock (Contract, "RFP frame"). Submission stays plain text until F-38.
  4. **Paging, search, filter and "all matching"** (G-26-2). The filter toolbar is drawn inert, as F-17 draws it. Select-all ticks only the rows on screen. A bulk accept sends `item_ids`, never a filter.
  5. **Where past answers come from, and who may read them** (G-26-5, G-24-4). Tola's past-answer line is fixture text. It says nothing about a past bid's result (Conflicts 2).
  6. **What Tola does with a rejection reason.** The reason is sent and kept internal. The page makes no claim that Tola learns from it (Conflicts 3).
  7. **Undo, "next: R-08", answer history, "Compare with v2", the response PDF and the clarification send** (G-26-4, -6, -7, -8). Not drawn unless returned; no part-1 fixture returns them.
- **Agent text is only displayed.** Tola's note, the "Suggested by Tola" rows and the suggested answers show exactly as returned and labelled as Tola's. None is a verified outcome, and none counts until the PM accepts it.

- **Request:** `{expected_revision: workspace.revision, step, item_ids, inputs?, idempotency_key}`, header `X-CSRFToken`. Built by the existing `useWorkspaceStep` and `stepRequest`. No new write path.
  - `accept`: `item_ids: ["R-07"]`, no `inputs`.
  - `accept_selected`: `item_ids` = exactly the ticked rows on screen, in row order. With nothing ticked the button is inert and sends nothing.
  - `edit_accept`: `inputs {answer (trimmed), status}`.
  - `write_own`: `inputs {answer, status, reason?}`. An empty reason is not sent. The `display` field is never sent.
  - `add_requirement`: `inputs {requirement, rfp_page}` (an integer).
  - The key is new per press and reused on a manual Try again of the same press.
- **200:**
  - `done`: the form closes, then the page re-reads (`.next.<step>`). The notice is the response's `message`. Focus goes to the suggestion panel's heading, or to the table's toolbar after a bulk accept. Rows, tags, counts and the next suggestion come only from the re-read.
  - `queued`: "Waiting: …", then a re-read.
  - `effect_uncertain`: the existing "being checked" notice. Every step is off, and nothing is sent again until the re-read says otherwise (W-3403).
- **Errors (F-17 rules):** 401 → signed-out. 403 → the forbidden notice and a re-read; 404 → not-open. 409 → the stale notice and a re-read. An open form keeps the typed answer, status and reason, and the next press sends the new revision. 422 → `fields[]` messages under Your answer, Status, Why not Tola's answer?, Requirement or RFP page, with the entries kept. 5xx, network or timeout → "Not done", a re-read, and Try again with the same key. None of these shows an accepted row.
- **Permission and tenant:** a control exists only where the response lists the step for that item (`items[].steps`) or for the workspace. The page never decides which rows may be accepted. Same data gives the same controls for every persona.

### Screen states (each page)
- **Loading:** the existing skeleton. **Empty:** a workspace with no sections draws the frame and "Nothing here yet." **Error:** the problem card with Retry. **Success:** the fixture states above.
- **Unknown and restricted:** a figure the response sends without a value reads "Unknown", never blank or 0. A `restricted` section, row or suggestion reads "Restricted": none of its text, past-answer line or steps is shown.
- **Not available:** a step with `disabled_reason` is a disabled button with that reason. It sends nothing and has no href.
