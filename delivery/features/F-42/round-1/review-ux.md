- UX-1-1 | blocking | medium | frontend | The three holds (W-3907, W-3908, W-3909) and the uncertain state (W-3911) draw a full-width "See all exceptions" button above the stations. Expected: holds read as a status, a reason ("NOT SET") and a Next sentence, with no button (spec criterion 6, "no button"; the Setup link is deliberately not drawn, G-C-3). Actual: a large button with a vague label that goes nowhere the hold explains. At 390 it sits directly on the mini station bar and clips it (see W-3907 and W-3909 at 390). Either remove it for these states or make it a plain, correctly spaced link with a label that says what it opens, and leave the station bar clear.
- UX-1-2 | blocking | medium | frontend | After "Approve register" on W-3902 the page shows "WAITING FOR DATE EXPLANATIONS" with the Next sentence "The 3 unclear in-service dates are explained and the proof attached. The date review starts then." The sentence says the explanation is already done, which contradicts the status, and station 3 reads "Needs a person" although nobody on the page can act and nothing says who explains the dates (G-55-1, no board). Expected (conflict 2 in spec.md): a clear waiting state with no dead end. Fix the sample `W-3902.approve.json` wording so it says what is still missing and who it waits for (for example "Waiting for someone to explain the 3 unclear dates and attach the proof. The date review starts after that."). The missing explain step itself stays with the design engineer (G-55-1).

Return and Reject state no consequence at all. Expected: each button is clear about what it does. - UX-1-4 | non-blocking | medium | frontend | The visually hidden "lifecycle" strip (DRAFTED, REVIEWED, APPROVED, SENT, RECEIPT RECORDED "Unknown", VERIFIED) is read by screen readers on every state. It comes from the generic `facts` array on the base item. Fix the fixtures or drop the strip on this line so screen-reader users hear the same story as the stations.
