## Findings
- UI-1-1 | blocking | medium | frontend | At 720px the top bar is wider than the viewport. The SAMPLE DATA label (`frontend/src/shell/TopNav.tsx:27`, `.psl-sample`) ends at x=725 on a 720px viewport, so `document.documentElement.scrollWidth` is 725 and the page scrolls sideways. Expected: no horizontal overflow at any width where the top bar is shown (720px and wider). Actual: overflow of 5px at 720px only; 740px and 760px are clean. Fix so the bar fits at 720px (for example, let the links compact or let the who-line and label give way earlier), then re-check 720 to 760px.
- UI-1-2 | blocking | medium | frontend (design-owner decision on the approach) | The signed-in name is not visible on the desktop bar from 720px to about 820px. `frontend/src/styles/shell.css:44-45` applies `overflow: hidden; text-overflow: ellipsis` to `.psl-nav__who`, but the element's measured width is 0px at 720 and 768px, 43px at 820px and 123px at 900px (finance_lead persona, five links). Expected: per CMP-01 and the spec's rule (f), the name shrinks first and remains visible as a truncated name. Actual: at 720 to about 815px no part of the name is shown, so the person cannot tell who they are signed in as on tablet-width desktops. The builder's note says this is the design owner's call (wrap, or a shorter who-line as the boards' `flex-wrap` allows). Frontend should not pick the approach alone; the design owner should choose it, then the frontend applies it and re-measures at 720, 768, 820 and 900px.
- UI-1-3 | blocking | medium | frontend | The error frame does not follow the approved STATES error pattern. `frontend/src/shell/FrameStates.tsx:55-66` renders a `psl-callout--problem` strip (pink fill, message in one line), then a plain-text "Reference not_found for support." line and a secondary button. The approved STATES-desktop and STATES-mobile boards show an error card: white surface, 2px ink border with a signal-red border, a mono eyebrow "ERROR · COULDN'T LOAD", the message, a "Last loaded … Reference … for support." line, and a bordered "Try again" button. Spec C5 names this as "STATES error pattern" and does not record a deviation as approved. Expected: the card layout and eyebrow from STATES. Actual: the callout strip, with the reference line not aligned to any board element. Also use the existing `.psl-error` or STATES card markup rather than a new one-off layout. Re-check the 404 and server error frames at 1440 and 390 against the board. (The loading frame was not captured; the mock responds too quickly to see it, so the loading pattern is unverified. See Notes.)
