## Findings
- QA-5-1 | blocking | high | frontend | Reopened. See `## Verified` for the exact steps and positions. Cause: `Workspace.tsx:310` adds `ws-decide--flow` only when `flow.allLocked && hasReconcile`, and `allLocked` is `useState(false)` (`useWorkspaceStep.ts:77`), set only by an `effect_uncertain` reply in the current session. A reloaded item that carries `reconcile` and `allowed_actions: ["reconcile"]` (`mock/fixtures/work/W-2008.next.json`) gets the fixed bar again. Separately, in the flowed state the `.ws:has(.ws-decide--flow) { padding-bottom: 0 }` rule leaves no room for F-09's fixed `.resp-actionbar`, so at maximum scroll the Decide panel (685-828 at 390) sits where the reconcile button is (776-826), and `elementFromPoint` returns `P.psl-reason ws-hint`. Expected: base the flow (and the lock) on the item's own reconcile state (`hasReconcile`), not only on session memory, and reserve the reconcile bar's height at the bottom of the page. Add the reload case to the phone e2e (press, `page.reload()`, tap) and a tap after scrolling to the bottom.
- QA-5-5 | blocking | high | frontend | **A reload re-enables a resend while delivery is uncertain.** Steps: persona finance_lead, any viewport (I checked 1440 and 390), `/work/W-2008`, press "Approve and send" (the mock replies `effect_uncertain` and the reconcile panel shows), reload the page, then press "Approve and send" again. Expected (spec criterion 19, outcome table row `effect_uncertain`, INV-RESEND): every step stays off while the item is waiting for reconcile, with no new key and no resend. Actual: after the reload, both Decide buttons have no `aria-disabled` and are enabled. The press sends a second `POST /api/v1/work/W-2008/steps` with a new key. Mock log: `[["approve_send",200,key aa6148cf,rev 3],["approve_send",200,key 1946d824,rev 4]]`. The page shows "Delivery uncertain" and "Confirm what happened before anything is resent." alongside the live send button. Cause: as in QA-5-1, `off()` (`Workspace.tsx:111`) reads `flow.allLocked`, which a reload clears. Expected fix: treat `hasReconcile` (item has `reconcile` and `allowed_actions` includes `reconcile`) as all-locked, with the "Confirm what happened first." hint. The backend must still refuse it (not provable on the mock).
- QA-5-3 | non-blocking | low | frontend | Carried forward, unchanged. On a phone, the uncertain callout "Delivery to Lagoon Health couldn't be confirmed." is below the fold after the press (y 938 on an 844-high viewport at 390).
