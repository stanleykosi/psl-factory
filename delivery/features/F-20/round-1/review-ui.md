## Findings
- UI-1-1 | blocking | medium | design engineer | Phone SCR-34 @390 visual 83.3 < 90, so the gate fails. What is forced: by my measurement from side-by-side.png, the line (about 22px), meta (about 22px) and full-width 44px Upload button (about 58px with margin) approved in Conflict 2 add about 100px to every card. That pushes card 2 down about 90px, card 3 about 180px, and so on. Conflict 7's three extra cards add only length below the board's frame. What is fixable: about 6px of padding per card, plus about 22px if the line and meta were joined into one muted line, e.g. "Invoice preparation and delivery · Version 1 · PDF". Neither change brings the cards back to the board's positions, so no compact card that still carries the approved elements reaches 90. The only way the builder could close the gap is a change the spec rejected or did not approve: drop the meta, or replace the full-width button with a link-height control on the tag row. Expected: the design engineer either (a) records a standing visual exception for SCR-34-mobile at 83.3, as was done for the SCR-01 phone gap, or (b) approves a compact card and the builder rebuilds it. A compact card would keep line + meta on one line and put a right-aligned "Upload PSL's version" link-button with a 44px target on the tag row. It could score in the high 80s, still unlikely to reach 90. I recommend (a). The builder should not be asked to keep cutting the approved elements to chase the score.
- UI-1-2 | blocking | low | builder | Desktop: "3 versions · Changed by Ada Okafor, 12 Nov" sits under "Upload PSL's version" in the action column (`setup-actioncell`, setup.css new block). In that column it reads as part of the action, not as the document's history. It is not where Conflict 4 (approved) puts it, and the board does not draw it there. It is also the only cause of the uneven Closure report row. Expected: keep version and kind inline after the name (see Notes), and put the history line as one muted `setup-meta` line under the name in the Document cell. Rows with `updated_by` grow by one line, which Conflict 4 accepted. Read-only rows (no Upload control) then show their history too. As built, history placed in the action column has no natural home on a read-only row; check `readonly.json`.
- UI-1-3 | non-blocking | low | design engineer | Phone "AS OF 12 NOV, 10:40" is at the end of the eight cards. Conflict 6 (approved) puts it under the note. At the end of the list, the time stamp for the data is about 1,500px below the first card. The builder's reason is that it moved the cards and lowered the score. Under UI-1-1 (a) the score is waived anyway, so I recommend putting it back under the note as approved. Under (b) the design engineer chooses.
- UI-1-4 | non-blocking | low | builder | Fixtures carry `updated_by`/`updated_at` only on Closure report, set that way to protect the desktop score (build.md). Real rows will usually have them, so the scored page under-represents real row height. That is acceptable for the score, but the mixed-data component test should keep covering every row with history, so the layout from UI-1-2 is exercised.

## Notes
- Conflict 4 deviation, version/kind: "Invoice  Version 1 · PDF" inline after the name keeps the drawn single-line rows and the four columns. It is closer to the board than the stacked lines the spec describes. I recommend the design engineer record it as the approved form of Conflict 4. "Version 1" with its label (rather than a bare "1") is clearer.
- `.setup-linkbtn` gets its 44px target from `margin: -14px 0`. On 37px rows, the hit areas of neighbouring rows' buttons overlap by about 7px. It meets WCAG 2.5.8 and is not a defect, but the edge of one target is clickable from the next row.
- Desktop count line: "8 TEMPLATES · 8 TEST  AS OF 12 NOV, 10:40" uses a gap, not the "·" separator, between the count and the time stamp. This is a matter of taste.
- The phone bar bell with an unread badge comes from the shell. The board does not draw it, the tool does not list it, and it matches the other Setup phone views.
