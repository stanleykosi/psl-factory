## What is sent, and what stays held (read first)

- **Sent (to the local mock only):** the review claim (E-TASK-CLAIM), one decision per press (E-TASK-DECIDE, verdict `approve`, `return` or `reject`, a note for return and reject), and one reconcile answer per press on an uncertain delivery (E-WORK-ACTION `action: "reconcile"`, with the proof upload E-EVIDENCE-UPLOAD only when the answer is "It arrived"). Nothing is published, sent to the CEO or anyone else, posted, or moved: the forecast moves no cash and authorises no payment.
- **Held, because a product or backend decision is open:** (1) who prepares the forecast: `finance.cash_forecast.preparer` is NOT SET (`holds_work: true`, `frontend/mock/fixtures/setup/finance.desktop.json:196-221`); (2) the inclusion rule: `finance.cash_forecast.inclusion` is NOT SET and read-only in Setup (`:222-238`); (3) who reviews the forecast: there is **no** Setup key for it (only preparer, inclusion and group reviewer exist), so this is a backend question and the page names no reviewer of its own; (4) what "Cancel delivery" does while the effect is uncertain, and which state follows (gaps `:137`); (5) whether publishing is automatic or a step, and who the "RECEIPTS 2 of 2" are (gaps `:136`, G-C-7); (6) per-recipient reconcile (G-C-5); (7) which verdicts the review offers (G-C-1); (8) the empty audience word in the basis sentence (G-56-9); (9) D-RETURN (does a return give the preparer a task). Every fixture is sample-marked; two hold fixtures show the backend's `setup_needed` for (1) and (2). The page shows whatever the re-read work item says and adds no rule.
- **Held to later parts:** group rows and group review (part 2: G-56-1 to G-56-4, D-PAYROLL-VISIBILITY, G-C-7), Record actuals and matching (part 3: G-56-5 to G-56-8, D-RECEIPT-ALLOCATION, D-DECISION-VARIANTS), Withdraw (`revoke`), Correct and resubmit after a return, the Start work forecast drawer (F-47B).

- **200:** the panel's existing "Recorded. Reloading the work item…" and a re-read; the page then draws the re-read state (W-4003 delivered → verified; not delivered → "NOT DELIVERED · HELD" with no resend; can't tell → "HELD"). "Verified", "Delivered" or "received" never appear from the 200 itself.
- **Errors:** 401 → signed-out; 404 → not-open view; 403 or 422 → the backend message in the panel; 409 → "This item changed…" with Reload; 5xx, network or timeout → "We couldn't confirm your answer…" with Reload, and nothing is sent again from the page.
