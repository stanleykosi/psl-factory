## What is sent, and what stays held (read first)

- **Sent (to the local mock only):** list reads with `q`, `status`, `cursor` and `limit` (E-INVOICES); on the reminder, one E-WORK-STEP per press for `send_reminder`, `edit_draft` or `set_aside`, and the reconcile answer (E-WORK-ACTION) after an uncertain send. The mock sends no email and writes nothing to any money record. Nothing reaches a client.
- **Held, because a product or backend decision is open:** (1) recording a payment received, its bank reference, proof and allocation (FE-PAYMENTS-RECEIPT, D-RECEIPT-ALLOCATION, D14 UNCONFIGURED): the "Record a payment received" button is not drawn; (2) what a short payment does and the link from a PART PAID row to that decision (D-DECISION-VARIANTS, G-SCR20-12): the row shows the backend's state and owed amount only; (3) the second-reminder interval ("If there's no reply in 7 days…", G-SCR32-5): no configured value exists, so the sentence is not shown; (4) who may send reminders and whether the PM is copied (gaps-e:176-178): shown only through the steps and recipients the backend returns; (5) a real send: waits on the backend's idempotency and resend refusal (BACKEND-HANDOFF priority 1). The page adds no rule for any of them.
- **Never:** a total, sum, balance, overdue amount, overdue count or days overdue worked out in the browser; a PAID or PART PAID state inferred from amounts; anything that reads as a debt cleared, written off or settled.

## Contract

### E-INVOICES `GET /api/v1/finance/invoices` (typed; read only)
- **Request:** `q` (the search text once it has at least 2 characters, after the 200 ms pause), `status` (one `filters[].value`), `cursor` (the returned `next_cursor`, for Show more only), `limit` 20. `client` is not sent by this page. `q` and `status` live in the address (`/finance/payments?q=…&status=…`); a reload sends the same query. A changed query drops earlier answers (request counter, as `useClients`).
- **200:** rows in returned order. Per row: reference (mono), WORK (Conflicts 4), INVOICED, RECEIVED, STILL OWED (each its own `{amount, currency, unknown}`; `unknown: true` or no amount reads "Unknown" in the library's amber `Unknown`, with "amount not known" spoken), STATE (`status.label` with `tagTone(status.tone)`; no status → "Unknown"), OVERDUE BY (Conflicts 3). A known `"0.00"` is "₦0.00". The reference is plain text (no row `work_id` is sent in these fixtures; when one is sent it links to `/work/<work_id>`). `sample: true` on a row draws `SampleLabel` on that row. Count: "<total> INVOICES" from `total`; no `total` → no count is drawn (never counted from the rows). `as_of` → "Updated 12 Nov 10:00" beside the count. Show more appears only when `next_cursor` is sent and appends that page; focus moves to the first new row.
- **Totals strip:** Conflicts 2. Amounts in full ("₦7,300,000.00", never "₦7.3M"), from the decimal string.
- **Errors:** 401 → `SignedOutState` with `next=/finance/payments`; 403 and 404 look the same (one "You can't open this page" state, no amount, client or count, no further call); 5xx or network → `WorkErrorCard` "We couldn't load the payments. Nothing was changed." with Try again. A Show more failure keeps the rows and shows the problem with Try again. A body without `items` or `as_of` is treated as an unexpected response.
- **Permission and tenant:** whatever rows the backend returns for this person; the page filters, hides and counts nothing itself. No role check, no `/me` read.

### E-WORK-STEP `POST /api/v1/work/{work_id}/steps` (send, edit, set aside)
- **Request:** `{expected_revision: workspace.revision as read, step: "send_reminder" | "edit_draft" | "set_aside", inputs: {} | {subject, message}, idempotency_key: <UUID, new per press, the same on a manual retry of that press>}`, header `X-CSRFToken`, through `useWorkspaceStep` only. One write per press; a double press sends one.
- **Edit:** the form opens on press with the returned values; Subject and Message are required (checked before sending, message under the field, announced, focus on it); Cancel sends nothing.
- **200 `done`:** re-read; the page draws what the re-read says (sent with receipts, Draft v2, or set aside). "Sent" or "Delivered" never comes from the click.
- **Errors:** 401 signed-out; 403 the read-only view after a re-read; 409 the stale sentence, the newer draft shown, nothing resent until a new press; 422 under the named field, entries kept; for a step that sends, a 5xx, network failure or timeout re-reads first and keeps Send off ("We are checking what happened. Nothing was sent again."); `effect_uncertain` → the uncertain notice and reconcile panel. Every step stays off while the item is `effect_uncertain` or carries `reconcile`, including after a reload (INV-RESEND, F-17 D-67).
- **Reconcile (E-WORK-ACTION):** offered only when `reconcile` is present and `allowed_actions` includes `reconcile`; the outcome comes from the item's `reconcile.outcomes`; after it, the re-read decides what is shown. No resend from reconcile.
- **Permission and tenant:** a control exists only when its step is returned; a step with `disabled_reason` is drawn off with that sentence and sends nothing. No role or person rule in the page; `/me` is not read.

### Screen states
- **List:** loading (`LoadingState`); empty ("No invoices to show yet."); no matches for a search or filter ("No invoices match", Clear filters); error (problem card with Try again); success (the board and variants); restricted (403/404 view).
- **Reminder:** loading skeleton; error card; success (W-3801 and the table); sent, delivered, set aside, uncertain, uncertain without reconcile, disabled send, read only.
