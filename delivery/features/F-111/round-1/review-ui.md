- UI-1-1 | blocking | medium | frontend | The Blocks fact is drawn as a separate panel under a thick rule, away from Reference, Severity, Status, Owner, Raised and Age (all four states, both widths; for example /work/5101). The board puts Owner, Raised, Age and Blocks in one strip, with Blocks as the highlighted (red-tinted) cell. - UI-1-2 | blocking | low | frontend | "Time unknown" is printed on the right of every Asked and Reminder row in Chasing (5101, 5102, 5107), next to a "Receipt recorded ..." line. The spec's Chasing mapping lists kind, status, receipt word and finding, and says never to show placeholders; the message item has no time field. Remove the right-hand "Time unknown", or show the receipt time there only when it exists.
- UI-1-3 | blocking | low | frontend | Banner tone. On 5102 and 5103 the board draws a red-tinted alert (warning icon, red text) for no response and no owner; the build shows a plain line (5102 "No response since 09 Nov" in the unstyled section) and an amber banner (5103). Use the same alert tone the board draws (the shared red alert style), or ask the design engineer to accept amber.
- UI-1-4 | blocking | low | frontend | The frame heading says "Waiting ... Next step. Wait for the owner to update the issue." above the adapter's own banner on 5102, 5103 and 5104, which contradicts it ("Chase the owner", "Choose an owner", "Nothing more to do" next to "The fix was confirmed"). Two competing next-step lines is a visible inconsistency. This is frame text (lane C); the adapter should make its own lead consistent or lane C should take `next_action` as the frame next step. Route to the coordinator; raise as a dependency on lane C if the builder cannot change it.
