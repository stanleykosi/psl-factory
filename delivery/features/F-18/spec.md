# Team input and the RFP outcome are recorded inside the RFP frame, and the drafted follow-up goes only when a person sends it

> Tasks: FE-M2B-RFP (F-09B; R-RESPONSE; acceptance A-RFP-OUTCOME, A-RFP-FOLLOWUP frontend halves, A-ACCESSIBILITY). Architecture: package `architecture/packages/v7`, manifest sha256 `f6231d20…4b624` (`manifest.sha256`); D-RFP-OUTCOME r2 and D-RESPONSE-VIEW r2. Design: approved canvas version 1794581312-b166, design_sha256 `9fad8d2d…f95c8cd` (`design/review.json`; the coordinator ran `design.py check` on 14 Nov 2026: APPROVED). Screens: SCR-16, 18 artboards listed under Acceptance 1 and 2. Model: `delivery/features/F-16/spec.md`.

## Summary
- A PM can record **team input** on the existing response route (`/work/[id]/respond?task=<id>`, the F-09 form with a `team_input` variant) and, once the RFP is submitted, **record the RFP outcome** (won, lost, pending or unknown, with proof) on a new route `/work/[id]/outcome`. The recorded states (won, lost, pending, unknown) show from the returned `rfp_outcome.recorded` block.
- On an unknown outcome, the follow-up email the agent drafted can be reviewed, edited and **sent by a person**. An agent never sends; there is no automatic or background send, and no automatic retry of any write.
- Desktop pages sit inside an **RFP frame** (reference, title, status tag, PM, five tabs with counts) drawn only from `rfp` on the work item. At 390px the boards draw no frame, so the phone shows the frame's back link and bar note instead.
- Each of the 18 boards passes the fidelity tool on the contract mock at its own width (coverage 100% after approved exceptions, visual >= 90).
- Everything runs on the **contract mock only**. D-RFP-OUTCOME is still `proposed`; it is used under the design engineer's recorded exception (`architecture/sources/decisions-2026-11-14-v2.md` (e)-(g), `decisions-2026-11-15.md` (h)(i)): the backend developer may change or refuse any field, and nothing is released. The PR must not claim a real backend.
- Two commit slices: **A** frame, team input, outcome form, mock; **B** recorded states and the follow-up send.

### Operations (v7, unchanged; this feature builds against them)
| Operation | Request | 200 | Errors | Permission, tenant, idempotency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E-WORK-ITEM `GET /api/v1/work/{work_id}` | path id | WorkItem with `rfp`, `rfp_outcome`, `tasks[].response` | 401 signed-out callout with `next`; 403 and 404 `NoAccessState` (no id, title or client shown); 5xx or network `WorkErrorCard` with Try again | Backend decides per record. Nothing is inferred from `/me`. |
| E-EVIDENCE-UPLOAD | as F-09, `kind` = `tasks[].response.evidence_kind` (team input) or `rfp_outcome.evidence_kind` (outcome), `case_revision` | `{evidence_id, sha256, case_revision}` | as F-09 | Reuse `useEvidenceUpload` and `ProofField` from `features/response/index.ts`. |
| E-TASK-CLAIM | `{expected_revision}` = task revision, when `response.claim_required` | as F-09 | as F-09 | Team input only. The outcome and the follow-up have no claim. |
| E-RESPONSE `POST /api/v1/tasks/{task_id}/response` | `{expected_revision, who, action, responded_at, evidence_id, attested:true, team, estimate_person_days?, what, case_revision, note?}` + CSRF | `{task_id, work_state, revision?, recorded?, next?}` | 409 reload, keep entries; 422 server message shown, entries kept; 403 reload then read-only/not-open; network/5xx uncertain: one reload, no resend | No idempotency key; the double-press guard of `useResponse.record` is the protection. |
| E-RFP-OUTCOME `POST /api/v1/work/{work_id}/rfp-outcome` | `{expected_revision = item.revision, case_revision = item.case_revision, submission_id = rfp_outcome.submission.id, result, learned_at, record_as, evidence_id, note?, reason?, lessons?, awarded_amount?, expected_decision_on?, remind_at?}` + CSRF | `{work_id, result, state, revision, engagement_work_id?}` | 409 keep entries, reload; 422 names fields; 403 reload then no form; 401/404 as above; network/5xx uncertain: one reload, no resend | One write at a time. Not idempotent by key: a repeat after a success fails on `expected_revision` (409). No automatic retry. |
| E-RFP-FOLLOWUP `POST /api/v1/work/{work_id}/rfp-outcome/followup` | `{expected_revision = item.revision, version = followup.version, body? `lessons_items` (E-RFP-OUTCOME) is **not sent** (see Conflicts 9). The frontend adds no field, endpoint or local type for anything the schema lacks.

### What the page shows, and from where
Every element is drawn from the returned field. A missing field leaves its element out, never a placeholder, never board copy. Times are WAT (`Africa/Lagos`, `formatDay`/`formatClock` from `features/response/labels.ts`).

1. **RFP frame (desktop only)**, from `rfp`: eyebrow `rfp.reference`; `h1` = `rfp.client · rfp.title` (just `rfp.title` or `rfp.client` when one is missing); status tag `rfp.status.label`, tone chosen by `rfp.status.kind` (deadline, pending: amber; submitted: neutral; won: green; lost: red; unknown: neutral), nothing derived from `days_left` or `at`; PM tag `PM {rfp.pm.name}`; five tabs from `rfp.tabs[]` in the order returned, label `tab.label`, count `count` or `count/of` only when present.
   - **Tab behaviour (decided here).** The tab with `current: true` is a non-link marked `aria-current="page"`. `team_input` links to `/work/{tab.work_id ?? this id}`. `outcome` links to `/work/{tab.work_id ?? this id}/outcome` only when this page's item carries `rfp_outcome`. `requirements`, `pricing` and `submission` are unbuilt (SCR-26, SCR-27, SCR-40 belong to FE-RFP-RESPONSE and FE-RFP-SUBMIT): they render as **plain text**, not links, not buttons, not in the tab order, with their count. No `href="#"`, no disabled button.
   - The tabs are a `<nav aria-label="RFP">` of links, not a `role="tablist"` (the boards' role is an exception; a tablist needs panels).
   - When `rfp` is absent: no frame; the page shows the F-09 header (`Crumb`, task title or section title).
2. **Phone (390):** no frame. `usePhoneBack({label: 'RFP', href: '/work/{id}', note})`; `note` = the recorded result in capitals (`WON`, `LOST`, `PENDING`, `UNKNOWN`) on recorded states, `OUTCOME` on the outcome form, none on team input. `h1` = the section title ("Record team input", "Record the outcome", or the result word). Desktop has no phone bar link.
3. **Team input** (`tasks[].response.variant === 'team_input'`, `task.you_can_act`): `TeamInputForm` (new, in `features/response/`). Fields: Team (segmented from `response.teams[]`, name as returned, a "Given" marker where `given`), the sentence `response.blocking` above the form, Estimate (person-days, shown only for a selected team with `estimate_needed`; hint "Needed for the costing sheet."), What they gave (`what`), Proof (`ProofField`), Received (`responded_at`, `WhenField`), and, kept from F-09 because the request requires them, "Who gave it" (`who`; chosen automatically when `responders` has one entry, a select otherwise), the attestation checkbox and the note. Button "Record input" (`RESPONSE_LABELS.record.team_input`); busy "Recording…". Entry link on the work item: "Record team input" (`RESPONSE_LABELS.entry.team_input`); add `team_input` to `RECORDABLE`. `action` = `estimate` when the estimate field is shown and `estimate` is in `response.actions`, else `input` when offered; if neither is offered the form is not open (Assumptions).
4. **Outcome form** (`/work/[id]/outcome`), shown only when the item has `rfp_outcome` with non-empty `results` and `record_as`, and `rfp_outcome.recorded` is absent (or the person chose "Record the outcome" from a pending/unknown recorded view). Order: "Record the outcome" heading and the line "What {rfp.client} decided about our response." (only with a client); Submission (`submission.label` + `submission.delivery`, else "Sent {sent_at}"); Result (segmented, only values in `results`, none preselected); fields per chosen result:
   - all results: "When you learned it" (`learned_at`, WAT; for **unknown** the same field is labelled "Last contact with the client"), Proof (`evidence_id`), "You are recording as" (`record_as`; a select when two or more options, chosen automatically when one), Note (2000 cap, counter);
   - won: "Awarded amount" (`awarded_amount`);
   - lost: "Main reason they gave" (select from `reasons[]` when returned) and "Lessons (what we'd do differently)" (`lessons`);
   - pending: "Expected decision date" (`expected_decision_on`) and "Remind me on" (`remind_at`);
   - unknown: "Remind me on" (`remind_at`).
   Button labels: "Record won", "Record lost", "Record pending", "Save as unknown"; busy "Recording…". A person with no `rfp_outcome` block, empty `results` or empty `record_as` sees the not-open state with no form.
   - **Awarded amount.** Currency `NGN` (the product's currency, as `formatAmount` in `features/decide/format.ts`). Blank → `awarded_amount` omitted. A checkbox "Amount not known" → `{currency:'NGN', unknown:true}` with no `amount`. Otherwise `{currency:'NGN', amount:'40951000.00', unknown:false}`; separators and the ₦ sign are stripped, a whole number gets ".00", more than two decimals or text is refused before any request. A blank or unknown amount is never sent or shown as 0 or 0.00.
5. **Recorded states** (`rfp_outcome.recorded`, desktop frame as above): heading from `recorded.result` ("Won", "Lost", "Decision pending", "Outcome unknown"); "What's recorded" rows from `recorded.rows[]` (label, value, `at` as day); "Reminder for {reminder.holder.name}" with `reminder.at` (day and time); lost: lessons from `recorded.lessons[]` (category label as a tag, text), "Lessons · recorded by {lessons_by.name}", debrief proof (`debrief.label`, `debrief.reference`; a link to `/work/{debrief.work_id}` only when present), "Archived as {archive_reference}"; won: "Engagement {engagement.reference} created" and a link "Open engagement {reference}" to `/work/{engagement.work_id}` only when `work_id` is present; `recorded.next[]` lines under "Next". Links: lost "Back to projects" (`/projects`); pending and unknown "Record the outcome" (reveals the outcome form on the same route, shown only while `results` is non-empty); won "Open engagement". Wrong shapes are tolerated: an unknown `result` string reads "Outcome recorded".
6. **Follow-up** (`rfp_outcome.followup`, unknown state): card "Follow-up email" with the agent (`followup.agent`, avatar and name as `AGENT_LABELS`), the `checks[]` list with `done|now|next` marks and `source`, "To {followup.to}" exactly as returned, the draft `body`, version, and the line `followup.note` (the board's "It goes only if you send it" arrives here; if the field is absent the page shows "It goes only if you send it." because the page's rule is that a person sends, not the data's). "Send follow-up" only when `can_send` is true and `status === 'drafted'`; "Edit" only when `can_edit` is true and `status === 'drafted'`. Edit opens a textarea with the draft; it saves nothing; the edited text goes with the send as `body` only when it differs from the draft and is not blank. Statuses: `drafted` (buttons as above), `sending` ("Sending…", no buttons), `sent` ("Sent {sent_at}", no buttons), `effect_uncertain` (warning callout: "We couldn't confirm the follow-up was sent. It won't be sent again until it has been checked." plus "Check again", which only reloads; no Send, no Edit).
   - **Send flow.** One press: disable the button synchronously (a ref guard, so a double press makes one request), show "Sending…", POST with `expected_revision` = the item's `revision`, `version` = `followup.version`, an `idempotency_key` from `crypto.randomUUID()` generated once per (version, body) and **kept for any repeat press of the same version and text**, a new one only when the version or the text changes. 200: show the returned status, then reload the item once. 409: reload; keep the person's edited text in the open textarea, show the current draft under it as "Current draft (v{n})" and the notice "The draft changed. Check it before you send."; nothing resends. 403: reload; no Send; the server message shows. 422: the server message shows, the text is kept. Network or 5xx: show the uncertain notice, reload once, never resend; if the reload also fails every follow-up control stays off and "Try again" only reads the item; if the reload shows `drafted` with `can_send`, a new deliberate press may send again with the same key.
   - No timer, effect or load path may call E-RFP-FOLLOWUP; only a `click` handler does.

### Screen states
- **Loading:** `LoadingState`; phone back link "RFP" to `/work/{id}` (unknown before load: "Work item").
- **Error:** 5xx or network `WorkErrorCard` ("We couldn’t load this {response form|outcome}. Your work is safe; nothing was changed."); 401 signed-out callout with `next` = this page; 403 and 404 `NoAccessState` (id, title, client and reference never appear, tab title is generic: "Record a response · PSL Factory", "Record the outcome · PSL Factory").
- **Empty / not open:** `NotOpen` ("This response isn’t open here" / "This outcome isn’t open here") with "Open the work item", when the block, grant or options are missing.
- **Success:** the recorded states above; team input keeps F-09's `SuccessView` (no team-input success board exists, so none is fidelity-scored).
- **Notices** (stale, uncertain, unconfirmed, problem): the existing F-09 callouts; same words.

## Conflicts with the boards
Each place the contract, the existing code or this spec adds, moves or removes something a board draws, with a recommendation. The design engineer decides before the build; each is an exception candidate, provisional until signed.

1. **Phone frame.** Phone boards draw no RFP frame, only "← RFP", the page title and the bar note. Readiness said the frame is drawn from `rfp`. Recommend: frame at desktop only; the phone keeps the back link and note. No exception beyond the frame's own bar items.
2. **Title size.** Desktop boards draw 32px; the frame's `.shell-title` is 40px (26px at phone). Recommend: keep the frame's sizes (D-28, F-14 and F-16 precedent).
3. **Tab semantics.** Boards use `role="tablist"`. Recommend: a `<nav>` of links with `aria-current` (a tablist needs panels). Exception on role.
4. **Unbuilt tabs.** Requirements, Pricing, Submission link to screens that do not exist. Recommend: plain text with the count, not focusable. Alternative: disabled buttons (rejected: a disabled control is a dead control and axe-noisy).
5. **Team input extras.** The board draws no "Who gave it", confirmation checkbox, or note, but the request requires `who` and `attested: true`. Recommend: keep them, as F-09 did (one responder is chosen silently; the checkbox sits beside the proof). Extra elements, no exception; `who` appears only when several responders are returned.
6. **Team names on the phone.** The phone board abbreviates "Engineering" to "Eng."; no field carries an abbreviation. Recommend: the full returned name; exception for the text.
7. **Unknown outcome: proof.** The board says "No proof needed. Nothing has been heard yet." but `evidence_id` is required by the request schema. Recommend: keep the Proof field required for all four results; the design engineer may ask the backend developer for an optional `evidence_id` on unknown (D-RFP-OUTCOME r3), after which the field is dropped. Exception on the line "No proof needed…".
8. **Unknown outcome: last contact.** The board shows a fixed value ("26 Nov · submission email"); the request needs `learned_at`. Recommend: the field is the `learned_at` input, empty until the person fills it, labelled "Last contact with the client".
9. **Lessons.** The lost form draws one free-text box; the recorded state shows categorised lessons (`lesson_categories`). Recommend: send `lessons` (text) only and let the backend categorise; `lessons_items` and `lesson_categories` are used only to label returned lessons. Alternative: add a category select per lesson (a new control the board does not draw).
10. **Awarded amount.** The board draws a bare amount. The contract needs a currency and an explicit unknown. Recommend: NGN fixed, plus an "Amount not known" checkbox (extra element); blank sends nothing.
11. **"You are recording as" on the phone.** The phone boards omit it; the request requires `record_as`. Recommend: show the select on the phone only when two or more options are returned.
12. **Copy with no field.** The boards carry sentences the contract has no field for: the form's "What happens next:" paragraphs (route A engagement; archive and Tola suggestions; reminder; follow-up draft), "If it differs from costing v2, we flag it for the PM…", the recorded intros ("Won. The response, costing v2 and team estimates move with it…", "Tamarind Loans told us the decision is delayed to 12 Dec…"), "Nothing is deleted… Tola uses accepted answers as suggestions", and "from the client's debrief". Many state what the system will do, which is a backend rule. Recommend: not built; one exception per sentence. If the design engineer wants them, they need an r3 field (for example `rfp_outcome.consequences[]`), not frontend copy. The generic footnote "The client doesn't log in. You record their decision with proof, such as the award or regret letter. Only roles PSL allows can record outcomes." is kept as static text because it restates D-RFP-OUTCOME's own wording; confirm.
13. **Won: planning-packet card and "Ask Tola".** The won board shows an agent card with five steps; the contract carries only `engagement` and `recorded.next[]`. "Ask Tola" (won, unknown) has no endpoint. Recommend: neither built; exception. The unknown card keeps the agent, checks and note that the contract does carry.
14. **Follow-up states the boards do not draw.** `sending`, `sent` and `effect_uncertain`, the "Check again" link, the Edit textarea and "Current draft" are not drawn. Recommend: build them from design-system callouts and fields; the UI review judges them for consistency, not fidelity.
15. **Recorded lost heading.** Board: "Lost · archived with lessons"; won: "Won · handed off to engagement". Recommend: the result word from `recorded.result` only.
16. **Recorded pending and unknown re-open the form.** The boards' "Record the outcome" button implies the form is reachable again. Recommend: it reveals the outcome form on the same route while `results` is non-empty; won and lost never show it.

## Assumptions
- The outcome lives at `/work/[id]/outcome`, not on the response route: it posts to the work item, not a task, and has no task id.
- Team input needs `what`, a team, proof, a received time and (for a team with `estimate_needed`) an estimate in the form, though the schema marks `what` optional. Reason: the board labels them as the fields to fill; the mock and backend can refuse otherwise.
- `action` for team input is `estimate` when the estimate field is shown and `estimate` is offered, else `input`; the backend may refuse the mapping. Reason: the schema offers both values and the boards draw no action control.
- Given teams stay selectable and carry a "Given" marker; the backend refuses a repeat if it must. Reason: no source says a given team is locked.
- Frame tone by `rfp.status.kind` and the joined `client · title` heading are presentation. Reason: the boards draw them and the enum is the contract's own.
- NGN is the only currency offered. Reason: the product's amounts are naira (`formatAmount`); no currency list is returned.
- Follow-up status wording ("Sending…", "Sent {time}", the uncertain callout, "Check again") is new UI copy using the F-09 callout style. Reason: no board draws these states.
- An idempotency key is reused for a repeat press of the same version and text, a new one otherwise. Reason: the endpoint is idempotent on the key, so reuse is the safe repeat; the backend promise is unbuilt and verified only on the mock.
- `record_as`, `reason`, `results` and `lesson_categories` labels come from the block; the four result words ("Won", "Lost", "Pending", "Unknown") and heading words are fixed UI vocabulary. Reason: `results` is an enum with no labels.
- Ports 4621 to 4623 are free. Reason: F-16 used 4601 to 4603.
- Wrong or missing r2 fields are tolerated by leaving elements out. Reason: D-RFP-OUTCOME can change at any time.
