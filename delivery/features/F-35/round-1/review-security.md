## Findings
- SEC-1-1 | blocking | medium | frontend | INV-ACCESS (G-SCR52-3) in the agent's text. W-3210 is the partial-visibility fixture: figures "Projects covered 3 OF 5" and coverage "2 projects are left out because you can't see them." Its `agent.steps` were copied from the full fixture: "Gathered October's records" with source "5 PROJECTS · 41 MEETINGS · 12 ISSUES", "3 MILESTONES COMPLETE · 2 INVOICES VERIFIED", and "Drafted the escalations section" citing "ESC-0011 · ESC-0012", while "Open escalations" is Unknown. On the page this tells a viewer who can see 3 projects that Tola read 5 projects and how many meetings and issues they have, and it shows escalation ids that may belong to hidden projects. The spec allows only a count of left-out projects. This fixture is also the contract the backend is asked to build to. Expected: on W-3210, Tola's step sources describe only the records the viewer can see (for example "3 PROJECTS · …", escalation ids only from visible projects or none), or no source at all. The partial-visibility checks should also look at `agent.steps[].source` and text, not only at project titles. Actual: `frontend/mock/fixtures/work/W-3210.json` (agent steps); the tests `frontend/tests/leadership-report-mock.test.ts:113-123` and `frontend/tests/leadership-report.test.tsx:240-248` check only project titles, so they pass.
- SEC-1-2 | non-blocking | low | backend | What approving sends. The builder left `sends: true` off `approve_send` in W-3202, W-3209 and W-3210, which the spec table asked for. The reason given is that `Consequence` (`frontend/src/features/workspace/Steps.tsx:17-25`) turns `sends` into "This sends to the client." The person deciding is not misled on the mock: the button label "Approve and send" and `decision.basis` "Approving sends this exact version to the audience." both come from the backend and show in the panel (`InlineDecide.tsx:307-361`). The gap is with a real backend. If it sets `sends: true` on this line with no step `consequence`, the panel would say the report goes to the client, which is the wrong audience for an internal send. If it sends no `basis`, nothing but the button label says a send happens. `delivery/BACKEND-HANDOFF.md` already asks for audience wording for `sends` on a decision step. Keep that question open, and require that an approve step that sends carries a backend sentence saying who receives it.
