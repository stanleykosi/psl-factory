## Findings
- QA-3-1 | blocking | high | frontend | A stale decide by a second reviewer is not shown honestly, and the typed note is lost without warning. Steps (`qa/stale.cjs`, reproduced 3 times at 1440 and 390): reset; context A (persona pm) and context B (persona ceo) both open /work/W-3602, and both claim (200). B types "Second reviewer: wait for their reply first." in the note (on the phone, inside the Drawer). A presses "Escalate to the client", and A's page shows MESSAGE DRAFTED. B presses "Don't escalate". The mock answers B's `POST /api/v1/tasks/T-3602/decision` with 409 stale_revision. Expected (spec AC 11, and the brief): B sees a stale message that stays until B acts on it, and B's note is kept or shown back to B; B is never left looking at an outcome they did not choose. Actual: B's DOM timeline at 1440 is +307 ms "This review changed while you were reading it, so nothing was recorded. Review the latest", then at +411 ms the stale alert is gone and the status tag reads MESSAGE DRAFTED. There is no alert, no live region and no textarea. At 390 the Drawer closes and the stale message never renders: +145 ms goes straight to MESSAGE DRAFTED. B pressed "Don't escalate" and now sees MESSAGE DRAFTED with a "Send for review" button, with nothing saying that someone else escalated it or that B's note was not sent. The second variant (B presses "Escalate to the client") also loses the stale message, though the outcome happens to match. Cause: on `view.kind === 'stale'` InlineDecide reloads the page at once (frontend/src/features/workspace/InlineDecide.tsx:97-104). The re-read item has T-3602 `already_decided`, so the escalation rule removes the panel (frontend/src/features/work-item/WorkItemOverview.tsx:730). The stale view (InlineDecide.tsx:265-276) and the note state unmount with it. Note: with the scenario cookie 409 (where the re-read still serves the decide state), the stale view does stay, and "Review the latest" brings the note back (desktop textarea; phone, when the Drawer is reopened). So the existing component tests and the mock 409 test do not catch the real two-reviewer case. Fix direction: keep a page-level notice after a stale decide whose re-read removes the panel. For example: "Someone else decided this while you were reading it. Your decision and note were not recorded." Show the unsent note text, or keep it copyable. Add an e2e test with two contexts.
