## Findings
- UI-1-1 | blocking | medium | frontend | The status tag position depends on wrapping. Both desktop boards draw the status tag ("AWAITING PMO LEAD REVIEW", "RETURNED TO PM") on its own line under the title. The page puts the tag inside `.docrev__titlerow` after the tab bar (`DocumentReview.tsx:201`, `document-review.css:9`). On the default state it wraps under the title by chance. On the returned state it sits at the far right beside the tab bar (page x 1235, y 123; board x 88, y 179). Expected: render the tag on its own line directly under the title, outside the flex row, at every state and width.
- UI-1-2 | blocking | medium | frontend | The returned-state agent panel is restyled. Both returned boards draw Tola as one compact blue info strip: avatar, "**Tola** · PMO agent" inline, then the sentence and the action as a text link on the next line. The page uses `Panel kind="agent"` with a separate header "TOLA · PMO AGENT" (uppercase display type, its own rule) and a padded body (`DocumentParts.tsx:150-180`). That makes it about 2.5x taller (desktop 140px against 57px; phone 178px against 64px). The work-item frame already draws this strip as `Callout kind="info"` with the agent name in bold (`features/workspace/Workspace.tsx:36`, `ws-agentnote`). Expected: reuse that strip with the avatar, the name inline, the backend's lines, and the disabled "Ask Tola to draft v3" with "Not available yet" in place of the link (conflict 1 B).
- UI-1-3 | blocking | low | frontend | The disabled "Edit as v3" is sized and placed unlike the boards. On mobile-returned the board pins "Edit as v3" full-width in the bottom action bar, the same as Return/Approve on the default phone board. The page puts it in the page flow above the sections. Expected: on desktop, the button at its natural or board width, with "Not available yet" under it. On phone, use the same pinned bar (`.docrev__bar` / `PhoneActionBar`) for the disabled "Edit as v3" and its reason. The grey disabled look is the approved conflict 1 B and stays.
