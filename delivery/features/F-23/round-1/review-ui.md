## Answers to the coordinator's questions
1. **Desktop default 69.7.** The gaps F-23 can fix are UI-1-1 (the tab bar the board doesn't draw, about 60px of downward shift) and UI-1-2 (the stacked tick box). UI-1-1 alone should lift the top half of the page, where most mismatched cells are. Do the round's one fidelity run after both. The remaining gaps are frame items already listed in exceptions or covered by D-83 and earlier reviews:
   - the agent note as a full-width band, not a side card;
   - Meeting and Decisions swapping columns (Conflicts 6);
   - the Decide panel's green button plus the opened form repeating the consequence and the label (D-69, D-98 item 2);
   - Edit/Remove drawn as buttons, not links;
   - the "ACTION NEEDED" fallback tag;
   - the crumbs drawn as plain text.
2. **Conflicts 6.** For the words, the tool is wrong; the placement difference is real. The frozen DOM has `<li class="ws-row">…<span>Go-live stays planned…</span>…<span class="ws-ref">From Notes · 14:12</span>`. In `extract.mjs`, the ownership loop (around line 252) treats any `li` ancestor as already holding the text, even when that `li` has no text of its own. The `li` itself is not an inline group because it holds a label. So the text is never pulled out, and these items are neither matched nor "extra". What really differs is the placement (side column against the board's main column, the frame layout under D-62) and the style (the cites are uppercase mono "FROM NOTES · 14:12", the board has sans sentence case). Exceptions 5-8 should say "words present; tool does not read text inside a row `li`; placement is the frame layout" rather than imply they are missing. The tool's `li` rule belongs on the factory tool backlog.
3. **The F-24 phone bar.** Nothing moves for this page. F-24 changed `PhoneBar`/`AppShell` only to add an optional `onNote` button, which the work page doesn't pass, so it still renders the same `<span>`. In `shell.css`, `.shell-phonenote` gained `padding-left`, an ellipsis and `min-width: 0`, and the back link became `nowrap`/`flex: none`. "← Queue" and "MIN-0141" are short and the note is pushed right by its auto margin, so nothing shifts. I rebuilt the merged head (mock 4768, app 4769) and took screenshots at 390 of W-2301..W-2304. They show the same bar (← Queue · MIN-0141 · bell · Start, 56px, static) and the Decide bar fixed to the bottom (170/97/118px, page `padding-bottom` matching, `scrollWidth` 390). The pre-merge phone scores still stand.
