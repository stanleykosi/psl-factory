A 409 on an upload or on the start re-reads where the revision came from (E-LINE-OPTIONS or E-LINES, F-05 PMO-0-3) and keeps what was typed. Changing the project clears the proof already added, because it belongs to the old case, and the drawer says so. Start is disabled while an upload is in flight.

## Risk
High. The proof is append-only on a case and is chained into the write that creates work and starts agents. Getting the case or revision wrong (project versus fixed back-office case, or a stale revision after an upload) attaches evidence to the wrong record, and that is hard to undo (INV-REVISION, D-START-INTAKE (4)).
