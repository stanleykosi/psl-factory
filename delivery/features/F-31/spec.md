## What is sent, and what stays held (read first)
- **On the contract mock, nothing is sent to anyone.** No department request goes out, no project is really created, no planning line really starts, and no file leaves the machine. Every "sent", "registered", "created" and "in" word on these boards is fixture text, worded as a backend would return it. The PR and `build.md` must say so and must not claim a working engagement registration, department requests or project creation.
- **What the page does write (to the mock only):** the PM confirmation (E-WORK-ASSIGN), the plan evidence and department proof files (E-EVIDENCE-UPLOAD), the registration (E-WORK-STEP `register_engagement`) and one department's input (E-RESPONSE `team_input`). Each write is one deliberate press, carries CSRF and the revision as read, and is followed by a re-read. The page never says "registered", "confirmed" or "recorded" until the re-read says so.
- **Held, because a product or backend decision is open:**
  1. Whether the factory may send department requests itself, or the PM sends them (G-42-3, no decision id). Tola's "Sent requests … EMAIL · 08 DEC" is shown only as a line of Tola's checklist, never as a delivery receipt.
  2. Whether pricing always runs again after planning (G-42-5, design/screens.md 2.5). The estimate-changed finding is shown as returned. Nothing starts costing v3, and SCR-27 stays held by D-COSTING-RULE.
  3. Who accepts readiness and what makes planning verified (G-42-6). "Prepare readiness" is drawn only as the response sends it, disabled here with its returned reason. Pressing it is part 2.
  4. Who receives each department's requests, and where that is set (G-42-4). "Who receives this?" has no target yet (Conflicts 5).
  5. Whether the engagement asks again for a PM (G-41-2, an open PSL question). The confirm step is built as drawn and can be removed by the backend not sending it. Its wording is checked at product acceptance.
  6. How registration creates the project and the planning item, and how a half-done registration is recovered (G-41-5). The page shows the backend's message and re-reads. It links to the new project and planning item only from ids the re-read returns, and never sends again while the result is uncertain.
  7. Where plan evidence lives before the project exists (G-41-4, G-23-5). On the mock it is uploaded to the engagement work item's own case.
- **Used as they are (D-112):** the shared side-panel heading ("Decide", or "Next" when the response sends next lines) stands in for the boards' "PM for this engagement" and "Readiness" titles until the backend sends a panel title. The shared timeline row is not used: no part-1 board draws a timeline.

## Summary
- The engagement work item (line `engagement`) shows Tola's planning packet, with unknowns kept as Unknown, and lets the person the response allows confirm the PM, pre-filled from the RFP, from the backend's candidates only (E-WORK-ASSIGN). The registered PM then registers the engagement with plan evidence, start date and client project contact (E-EVIDENCE-UPLOAD, then E-WORK-STEP `register_engagement`).
- The planning work item (line `planning`, on project P-0106) shows what each department was asked for and where it stands, Tola's checklist, the estimate-changed finding and the Readiness panel. "Record input" on a waiting department opens F-18's team-input form in the shared Drawer and records that department's input, with proof, under the PM's own name (E-RESPONSE `team_input`).
- Every control is drawn only when the response sends it. No role, `/me` value or state name grants one. 403 and 404 look the same. Counts, totals, statuses and tones come from the backend. Unknown reads "Unknown", never blank or 0.
- Writes carry CSRF and `expected_revision` as read. The step also carries an `idempotency_key`, kept across a retry of the same press. A failed, timed-out or uncertain write never says "registered", "confirmed" or "recorded". The item is re-read first, and an uncertain registration is never sent again.
- New in this feature: the pre-filled PM form, the response drawer, the text-plus-money cell rule, four fixtures (W-2601 to W-2604) and their scripts, a planning line on P-0106, and the overlay `frontend/mock/proposals/engagement-planning.json`. Contract mock only.

### E-WORK-ASSIGN `POST /api/v1/work/{work_id}/assignment` (confirm PM)
- **Request:** `{expected_revision: workspace.revision, person_id: <one of assign.candidates[].person_id>, note?: string (≤ 2000)}`, header `X-CSRFToken`. Built by F-30's `useWorkspaceAssign`; no new write path. No idempotency key (not in the typed body). One send per press, and a double press sends one. After an uncertain result, Confirm is offered again only if the re-read still carries `assign`.
- **200:** `{work_id, state, revision, assigned_to}` → re-read. The register step and the "PM · Sola Martins" fact appear only from the re-read (`W-2601.next.json`). Notice "PM confirmed: <assigned_to>" when `assign.role` is "PM", otherwise F-30's "Owner set: …".
- **Errors:** 401 → signed-out. 403 and 404 → not-open, alike. 409 → re-read, the stale sentence, the choice kept, no automatic resend. 422 → `fields[]` messages under "PM" or "Note". 5xx, network or timeout → "Not confirmed", with Try again after a re-read.
- **Permission and tenant:** the form exists only when the response carries `assign` with at least one candidate. The page never filters candidates and never reads `/me` to choose or show a PM.

### E-WORK-STEP `POST /api/v1/work/{work_id}/steps` (register the engagement)

Built by the existing `useWorkspaceStep` and `stepRequest`. `inputs` holds only the fields the step lists, and a field left empty is not sent (never "0" and never an invented date).
