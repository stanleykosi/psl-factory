Correction actions

Supply a credit, debit or void note on the case with document_ref, invoice_reference, kind, amount, currency and amount_basis. Use null for an unknown credit/debit amount. A full void may cite null amount; the configured method binds the full current adjusted face. Supply separate cash-correction evidence if an authorised note must reverse live allocations. A successor statement retains the original source, transaction, customer, currency, basis and received_at and corrects only amount. Its human review and verification evidence are separate.
